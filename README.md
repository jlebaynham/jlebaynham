## How I got here

I started in **business analysis**. UK and Australia, consulting and in-house, sitting between the people who do the work and the people who buy the software.

Somewhere in there I spent a stretch in **general insurance broking**, which is why a lot of what I build points at brokers. I know exactly which parts of that job are a person retyping something a computer already knows.

The pattern was always the same. Write the requirements, hand them over, wait months, watch half of it arrive. Meanwhile the person doing the job is still copying an email into a spreadsheet.

**So now I build it myself.** Small things. One workflow, one tool, one integration that removes a job somebody hates doing, live this week rather than next quarter.

> *"The analyst's questions. The builder's hands."*

Four things, all small enough to ship fast and cheap enough to keep running.

<table>

<td width="50%" valign="top">

### Email and document workflows
`Messy inbox` > `Classify and route` > `Drafted and logged`

Workflows that read what comes in, sort it, label it, draft the reply and log it where you can see it. Built on n8n, running on my server or yours.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### AI tools that earn their place
`Real task` > `Claude in the loop` > `Output you can trust`

AI where it genuinely beats a rule, and plain code everywhere else. Guardrails on anything with a number or a name in it, because a confident wrong answer costs more than no answer.

</td>
</tr>
